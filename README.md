# GastroDiseaseDetection

## Version 1

Version 1 of this project uses the [Gastrointestinal Disease Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amanik000/gastrointestinal-disease-dataset) from Kaggle.
The dataset is located locally at `datasets/gastrointestinal_disease_dataset.csv`.

### About the Dataset

This dataset is designed to aid research and analysis in the field of gastrointestinal and autoimmune diseases. It includes medical history, genetic predisposition, lifestyle factors, and clinical indicators for predicting and understanding disease classification. 

Key attributes include:
- **Demographic & Physical:** Age, Gender, BMI, Body Weight, Obesity Status, Ethnicity, Family History.
- **Biological & Genetic:** Genetic Markers, Microbiome Index.
- **Autoimmune & Inflammatory:** Autoimmune Disorders, H. Pylori Status, Fecal Calprotectin, Occult Blood Test, CRP/ESR.
- **Diagnostic Results:** Endoscopy Result, Colonoscopy Result, Stool Culture.
- **Lifestyle & Diet:** Diet Type, Food Intolerance, Smoking Status, Alcohol Use, Stress Level, Physical Activity.
- **Symptoms & GI Issues:** Abdominal Pain, Bloating, Diarrhoea, Constipation, Rectal Bleeding, Appetite Loss, Weight Loss, Bowel Habits, Bowel Movement Frequency.
- **Medication Usage:** NSAID Use, Antibiotic Use, PPI Use, Other Medications.
- **Target Variable:** Disease Class (e.g., Crohn’s Disease, Ulcerative Colitis, IBS, Healthy).
